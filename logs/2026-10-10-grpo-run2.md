## Interpretation
- Clear, statistically solid gain: invalid episodes fell from 43% to 4%, which roughly doubled all-rows EM on both splits.
- The gain is not only "fewer failures": on completed episodes EM, F1 and citation precision also rose, and searches went **up** slightly (1.9 → 2.3), so there is no shortcut collapse on evaluation.
- Evidence coverage stayed flat (~0.47). The model cites more precisely but does not cite more of the needed sentences. The mid-training dip in evidence coverage (0.48 → 0.41) did not show up in evaluation.
- Validation EM is higher than holdout EM for both models (train-source questions vs. dev-source questions); compare within a split only.
- Decision: run 2 settings (256 q, G=8, lr 2e-5) are adopted for the R0/R1/R2 comparison. The prompt fix is **not** needed, since invented citations were nearly eliminated by training. Run 2 is effectively **R1, seed 7**.
