## Interpretation
- The worked example mostly fixes invented citations (evidence failures 85 → 20). That is where the extra 53 completed episodes come from, and so the +6 pt EM and grounded EM.
- Protocol failures go up (37 → 54). Most are "actions must not contain newlines": the model copies the demonstration's layout into multi-line actions.
- Answer quality on completed episodes is unchanged (EM 38–39%, F1 ≈ 0.50). B0-FS is still far below the GRPO runs (EM 38–49%, grounded EM up to 39%), so prompting alone does not explain the RL gains.
